# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React, Vite, and TypeScript. Framer Motion controls beat choreography and shared-element motion. The production build emits one standalone HTML file.

## Users

The primary audience is partners and investors evaluating an information-first heritage platform. The product serves international travelers who want trustworthy context at a physical place, in their language, at the moment of discovery.

## Product purpose

A visitor scans a QR marker at a heritage place. The product presents curated history, cultural meaning, architecture, etiquette, and stories. It works online with rich media and planning, and it preserves a small essential knowledge capsule for limited connectivity.

## Positioning

The product separates verified facts, scholarly interpretation, oral accounts, and legend at claim level. AI translates, summarizes, explains, personalizes, and plans from that verified base. AI does not decide historical truth.

## Operating context

Visitors use a phone at monuments, temples, artifacts, and cultural sites. Partners evaluate provenance, multilingual access, offline behavior, community contribution controls, trip planning, and affiliate revenue from useful next actions.

## Capabilities and constraints

- A scan opens language-aware editorial content, audio, sources, questions, and planning.
- The offline QR payload is a compressed knowledge capsule decoded by the scanner, app, or PWA. It does not claim to load a full web page through a normal camera without prior software or connectivity.
- A standard QR code can theoretically hold about 2,953 binary bytes at its densest version. Scannability and error correction reduce practical capacity.
- Community submissions require evidence checks, comparison, and human or local review before they can become canonical.
- Monetization begins after the information experience through clearly marked affiliate or sponsored next actions.
- The presentation must work as a 16:9 full-viewport keynote with deterministic beat navigation, deep links, reduced motion, presenter notes, and intentional video fallbacks.

## Brand commitments

The presentation uses a near-black and warm-white palette with one restrained amber or terracotta signal. The voice is direct and evidence-led. The visual bar is a cinematic Apple product keynote, with full-bleed heritage imagery, large type, negative space, and motion that explains transformation.

## Evidence on hand

`BUILD_BRIEF.md` supplies the product narrative, technical caveats, slide sequence, interaction model, and public claims to verify. The repository starts without product screenshots, customer evidence, traction, or financial results. Future work must not fabricate them.

## Product principles

- Authority comes from verified sources and accountable review.
- AI stays downstream of the knowledge base.
- Essential context survives weak connectivity.
- The same canonical knowledge can adapt to language and visitor intent.
- Commerce follows understanding and stays visibly separate from editorial truth.

## Accessibility & inclusion

Keyboard controls, visible focus, responsive projector layouts, and a coherent `prefers-reduced-motion` mode are required. Multilingual content must preserve source meaning and remain legible across scripts.
